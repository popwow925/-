## What we collect
**The developer collects no data.** There are no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## Data that moves between your device and your friends' devices only
- Your **nickname** and **avatar** (shape, face, color) are stored on your own device.
- When you join a room, your nickname, avatar and game actions (scores, votes, bets) are sent over the **local network (same Wi-Fi or peer-to-peer)** to the host's device and the other players' devices. They are not sent to the internet or to the developer.
- Room data is cleared when the game ends or the room is closed. Reports are kept in the host device's memory only and are cleared when the game ends.

## Permissions
- **Local Network**: used to find and connect to friends' devices on the same network. If you decline, the offline “Solo demo” still works.
- This version does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data.

## Content safety
Hosts can remove players; any player can report a nickname or hide it on their own device. Strong interactions are off by default.

## Children
The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
Updates are published on this page with a new effective date.
